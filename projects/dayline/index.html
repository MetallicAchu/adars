How it works

Family Link and YouTube come in overnight.

The collector stores the night through the cutoff.

A night that was not stored is left as a gap.

Grok writes the note from that stored night, and the day is not marked complete.

Where the app list comes from

App time comes from Family Link.

A new app is flagged against the last stored day.

Each row is the time on that app for the stored night.

The list is a record. Nothing is changed on the device.

What the flag means

Gemini reads the video and writes the reason.

Needs a look is not a no.

The reason sits on the row.

Grok writes the evening note. Grok does not judge the video.

AI does the repetitive reading.
The system keeps the context.
